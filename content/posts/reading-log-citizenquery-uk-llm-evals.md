<!-- WHAT IT IS (2–3 sentences)
22k citizen query-response pairs grounded in 2,800 gov.uk pages (16 domains: benefits,
taxes, immigration, etc.). Scores LLM responses on factuality, abstention, and verbosity.
-->

<!-- THE INTERESTING PART: SYNTHETIC GROUND TRUTH

The expected answers are not human-authored — generated by Qwen 2.5 72B from the corpus pages.
Factuality scoring works like this:

1. Expected answer  → decomposed into atomic reference facts
2. Model's actual answer → decomposed into atomic claims
3. Llama 3.1 8B judge checks each claim for entailment against the reference facts
4. Score: F1@K (precision + recall over atomic facts)

Note: "supported" ≠ "true" — a claim is only checked against the synthetic expected answer,
not the corpus directly.
-->

<!-- CONTRAST WITH OTHER APPROACHES (2 sentences)
SAFE and FActScore compare claims against a knowledge base (SAFE uses the internet).
This paper compares against a synthetically generated expected answer — cheaper, but quality
depends entirely on the generation step.
-->

<!-- TENSION / OPEN QUESTION
